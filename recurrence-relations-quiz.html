Self Assessment: Recurrence Relations

A recurrence relation for a sequence *a*~0~, *a*~1~, *a*~2~, *a*~3~, . . . is an equation that relates *a*~*n*~ to one or more previous terms in the sequence. An initial condition is a value for the first term (or terms) in the sequence.
A formula for a recurrence relation is a rule that allows you to evaluate *a*~*n*~ directly.
